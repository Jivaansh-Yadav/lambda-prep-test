# Lambda Prep

Create the web portal for Lambda Prep (Lambda Education Pvt. Ltd.).

Brand & Legal:
- Platform Name: Lambda Prep (display mark lowercase 'lambda' with Greek λ)
- Legal copyright colophon in footer: '© 2026 Lambda Education Pvt. Ltd. All rights reserved.'
- Logo: Half-Life authentic Greek lambda (λ) with circular border and electric blue gradient (attached SVG).
- Aesthetic: Corporate, institutional, refined editorial, clean white, slate, and deep navy with electric blue accents. Zero generic AI slop.

Navigation Bar:
- Brand logo on the left with 'lambda'
- Direct horizontal grade selector on the right: 'ICSE : IX   X  |  ISC : XI   XII' with direct clickable pill links to the class hubs (refer to attached wireframe image-2.png)
- Quick links for Resources, Syllabus, and Sign In

Homepage Sections (in exact order):
1. Hero Section:
   - Left side:
     - Tagline: 'Discover premium content for your academics'
     - Main headline: 'Your ultimate school resource finder!'
     - Description: Curated and organized study materials, revision notes, and exam practice papers tailored for board mastery.
     - Verification checkmarks: '[✓] Curated content' and '[✓] Real testimonials'
     - Primary action buttons: 'Explore resources' and 'View syllabus'
   - Right side: Clean, minimalist vector illustration of a student studying with generous whitespace (no card overlays or clutter).
2. Metrics Strip (anchored directly under hero):
   - '50k+ students' | '10k+ resources' | '100% results'
3. 'Choose Your Class' Section:
   - 4 cards: Class 9 (ICSE Foundation), Class 10 (ICSE Board Prep), Class 11 (ISC Core Streams), Class 12 (ISC Board Finals).
   - Clicking each card routes to its dedicated page (/classes/icse-9, /classes/icse-10, /classes/isc-11, /classes/isc-12).
4. 'How It Works: Community Resource Sharing':
   - 3-step numbered workflow: 1. Select Stream & Subject, 2. Discover Quality Content, 3. Master Finals with verified solutions.
5. Corporate Manifesto / Mission:
   - Clean, signal-dense education free of noise by Lambda Education Pvt. Ltd.
6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):
   - Clean accordion covering syllabus alignment (2025–26), prelim papers, and community verification.
7. Footer:
   - Corporate colophon, quick links, and '© 2026 Lambda Education Pvt. Ltd. All rights reserved.'

Dedicated Class Hubs (e.g. /classes/icse-10):
- Display square cards for all subjects with colored icon squircle containers and subject names inside (refer to attached image-3.png):
  - Biology (Leaf icon)
  - Chemistry (Conical flask icon)
  - Commercial Applications (Calculator icon)
  - Computer Applications (Laptop/Screen icon)

  - History & Civics (Pillars icon)
  - Mathematics (Ruler icon)

- For ISC (Classes 11 & 12): include stream filter tabs (All, Science, Commerce, Humanities).
